## What We Built

Pacific Data Commons is an x402-gated data marketplace for Pacific institutions — fisheries agencies, climate research bodies, trade ministries, universities, and cultural organisations.

Data providers list queryable API endpoints. AI agents and researchers pay USDC directly to the provider's Algorand wallet — per query, instantly, with no intermediary holding funds. Synergy Blockchain Pacific operates the directory and the payment rail. We never touch the data and we never touch the money.

> "Pacific data is some of the most strategically valuable data on the planet. Pacific institutions that generate it rarely capture any of that value. Pacific Data Commons fixes that."

## Why It Exists

Ocean temperature readings, fisheries stock assessments, trade flows, remittance corridors, climate projections — this data shapes policy decisions worth billions of dollars annually. Yet the Pacific institutions that generate it operate on development grants and government budgets, with no mechanism to monetise their data assets internationally.

The problem isn't the data. It's the infrastructure to monetise it sovereignly.

A fisheries agency in Samoa can list an endpoint on Pacific Data Commons today and receive USDC payments from an AI agent in Singapore tomorrow — with no bank account approval, no payment processor, and no intermediary taking a cut beyond a 3% directory fee that SBP invoices monthly.

The provider keeps 97%. The data never leaves their infrastructure. SBP is the rail, not the repository.

## How It Works

Every endpoint listed on Pacific Data Commons:

- Is identity-verified at the institutional level before listing
- Has a machine-readable skills file so AI agents can discover and query it automatically
- Pays the provider directly on every query via the x402 protocol on Algorand
- Records an on-chain provenance certificate anchoring the dataset hash at time of certification

The x402 protocol is the technical foundation — a standard for HTTP-native micropayments that allows any AI agent or automated system to pay for API access per-query, without a subscription, without a credit card, and without a human in the loop. We're running this on Algorand Mainnet with USDC settlement through the GoPlausible facilitator.

## The Trust Tier System

Pacific Data Commons uses a three-tier trust system that SBP maintains without ever assessing data quality.

**Bronze — Identity Verified.** SBP checks institutional email domain, confirms the institution in an official registry, and verifies the contact person. Free, same day. This is who they are — not how good the data is.

**Silver — Community Verified.** Three verified purchaser wallets submit upvotes after paying for queries. The community rates — SBP records. No quality judgment from us.

**Gold — Peer Reviewed.** Provider submits a DOI or peer review evidence. SBP checks it resolves and the author matches — 15 minutes of clerical work. We confirm the claim; we don't replicate the review.

This distinction matters. SBP is a directory and a rail. Every trust signal comes from the community or the provider's own documented evidence — never from SBP's opinion.
